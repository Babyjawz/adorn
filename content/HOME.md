# Welcome to Adorn

This wiki covers **Adorn** and **Adorn Seller Framework (ASF)**: what each mod does, how to use it, and how to create and share your own content.

The contents on the left organise the guides by mod and purpose. The list on the right follows the headings of the page you are reading.

## Adorn

**Adorn is an in-game outfit studio for your character, NPCs and factions.** Browse the clothing and armour supplied by your installed mods, combine pieces into custom looks and inspect them in a rotatable 3D preview. Give a follower their own style, coordinate a group, keep a personal wardrobe and share assignment contributions with other players.

You can start with an installed set, replace a few pieces or assemble a look from several mods. Compare a recipient’s current outfit with your proposal, save the assignment and return to it later to edit, disable or remove it. Personal saved looks stay with the loaded save; NPC and faction rules are shared configuration.

## Adorn Seller Framework (ASF)

**Adorn Seller Framework (ASF) connects those looks to a seller, a challenge and a reward.** Find the assigned character, solve their request and earn finished pieces or learn to craft your selection—even supported pieces whose original mod has no recipe. Its in-game author tools let creators configure the seller, write and record the conversation, build native dialogue, and choose the crafting and riddle rules.

A tailor, a demanding collector, or a **follower with an unusual side business**.

For players, ASF makes acquiring a chosen look part of play. For NPC authors and modlist creators, it provides a way to build a service around a character: their exchanges, the performance behind each reply, the requests they make and the crafting rules attached to their rewards. Prepared conversations can combine stock Skyrim recordings, personal takes and silent replies.

## Author tools

Create the content used by ASF: recipe instructions and materials, item-payment riddles, optional hints and prepared seller replies. The author guides explain how these parts fit together before taking you through each editor.

Recipes define the separate cost of crafting each reward piece. Riddles define the seller’s item payment, with optional reward tiers and an extra clue. Authors can write manually or request optional OpenRouter suggestions, review the result and decide what to save. The dialogue builder prepares matching native records and audio for the next launch.
