## Adorn Seller Framework

Give selected looks a place in the adventure. Find the assigned seller, solve their request and earn finished pieces or learn to craft them. Authors can create the conversation, record replies and set the recipe and challenge rules.

A tailor, a demanding collector, or a **follower with an unusual side business**.
