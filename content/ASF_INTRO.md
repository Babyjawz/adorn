# Introduction to Adorn Seller Framework (ASF)

**Adorn Seller Framework (ASF) gives the outfits you choose in Adorn a place in your adventure—and gives authors the tools to create the character who provides them.** Pick a look, find the assigned seller, solve their request and return with the answer items. Earn the finished pieces when enabled, or receive a named parchment and learn to craft the outfit yourself.

A tailor, a demanding collector, or a **follower with an unusual side business**. ASF supplies the order and reward system; the author's choices give that service its personality, conversation and crafting rules.

## An outfit worth working towards

Start with an installed outfit or a custom combination assembled in Adorn. The first order points you towards the assigned NPC, and speaking through their outfit-planning dialogue introduces the seller before revealing the payment riddle.

An authored request can require **one to eight distinct miscellaneous items**, with quantities that must all be satisfied. Bring the complete collection back to the seller for hand-in. The request belongs to the accepted order, so later author edits do not rewrite the deal you are already pursuing.

With seller mode active, Adorn's ordinary direct-grant route gives way to ordering. Wearing a saved look checks that the player owns its pieces. The wardrobe remains convenient while acquiring its contents becomes part of play.

## Craft the look you selected

ASF can generate native crafting entries for **supported selected clothing and armour pieces even when their original mod supplies no crafting recipes**. It uses Adorn-owned recipe slots without copying or editing the outfit mod's original recipes. The mods supplying the pieces still remain required.

Recipe rewards arrive as distinct named parchments. Read the note to learn that outfit's entries, then collect its separate crafting materials. Previously learned outfits remain craftable as later orders are completed, within the shared **32 craftable-piece slots per save**.

Authors choose materials per piece, parchment instructions and optional named station locations. Armour normally uses a forge and clothing a tanning rack. They can also allow finished-outfit orders, which deliver the selected pieces directly and use no recipe slots.

## Give a character the seller role

Assign a **placed NPC** as the seller: an existing character, a suitable follower or someone supplied by a compatible content pack. The generic framework requires no particular follower, and the NPC does not need a merchant inventory for these orders.

One seller is active at a time, while saved conversation drafts can cover several NPCs. The service's dialogue sits alongside the character's ordinary merchant, follower and quest conversations; it does not replace their entire voice or personality.

## Write and perform the conversation

The in-game dialogue editor covers **seven core seller exchanges**, from opening the planner to hand-in, delivery and pending-order banter. Write the seller's reply, give the player compatible alternative prompts and save a draft for that NPC.

For the performance, choose from **42 stock Skyrim voice styles with seven catalogued replies each—294 recording candidates**. You can audition a reply before choosing it, seed a conversation from a style and replace individual moments.

You can also **record through a microphone inside Skyrim**, import supported audio files and trim a selected take using its waveform. Mix stock recordings, your own performances and silent subtitle/lip replies across the conversation. The original takes stay available.

The **Build** workflow generates the matching NPC-conditioned dialogue records, voice filenames, FUZ/lip files and supporting output. Authors prepare the conversation in game, let the build finish, then exit for installation and restart to activate changed dialogue. They do not manually create each dialogue record or calculate its audio filename.

## Author the challenge

The riddle maker starts with real answer items and quantities. Write the initial riddle, optionally add an extra clue, and set deduction difficulty and acquisition effort separately. Prepare the extra clue as a recorded or silent native reply so a stuck player can ask the seller for help on their accepted order.

Optional **Easy, Medium and Hard** reward tiers connect eligible riddles to classified reward pieces. Tiered offers are remembered within the save, preventing cancellation from repeatedly rerolling the same deal. Tiers are off by default; authors must supply and review a suitable progression pool.

**OpenRouter writing assistance is optional.** It can suggest wording for the chosen answer items, which the author reviews and saves. Ordinary gameplay and manual authoring work offline. Players using prepared content need no API key.

## For players and content authors

Players can use a prepared seller setup and follow the order loop. NPC authors and modlist creators can configure the seller, write the conversation, record replies, choose recipe rules and build a pack for those players.

ASF requires **Adorn** and its listed dependencies, plus its own runtime requirements. Authors building changed dialogue need the additional author tools; players using prepared output do not. The [requirements guide](../SELLER_NEXUS_REQUIREMENTS.md) separates the two roles.

The [compatibility and limits guide](../STATUS.md) explains recipe capacity, the active seller role and the restart required after building changed dialogue.
