# Introduction to Adorn

**Adorn is an in-game outfit studio for your character, NPCs and factions.** It brings the clothing and armour in your installed mods into one place to browse, combine, preview and assign. You can give a favourite follower a signature look, coordinate a faction, or build a personal wardrobe without leaving Skyrim to manage each outfit change.

Adorn works with the items and meshes your modlist already supplies. The creative choices are yours: start with a complete installed set, replace a few pieces, or assemble a new combination from several mods.

## Explore your collection

Browse installed plugin outfits, search individual pieces by name, EditorID or source plugin, and explore detected BodySlide groups where supported. A complete set can be a useful starting point; an individual piece can finish a look you already have in mind.

Custom looks support **up to 32 pieces**. Add a full set, remove what you do not want, and mix compatible clothing, armour and accessories. Source labels and build warnings help you understand what is available and which meshes are being used.

## See what you are choosing

The rotatable **3D preview uses your installed outfit meshes**. Inspect a combination as you build it and select the preview body sex. For a selected recipient, **Their outfit / Your outfit** lets you compare their current look with the one you are preparing.

Previewing is part of the design process; saving an assignment is the step that applies your decision. Mesh compatibility, equipment slots and clipping still depend on the pieces you choose. Adorn does not build missing BodySlide meshes or convert clothing between bodies.

## Dress characters and groups

Find a recipient through nearby actors, the crosshair or the active-modlist search. Assign a look to an NPC, a faction, or a named group containing several recipients.

Faction rules can keep individual exclusions, while direct NPC assignments take priority over faction assignments. That lets you give a group a common identity and still keep a particular character's own style. NPC assignments target the character's base record, so copies made from that base can share the result.

The **Assignments** view keeps those decisions together. Search, edit, disable or remove a rule as your plans change. Saved assignments can dress eligible nearby actors and generate SkyPatcher rules for the next launch.

## Keep a personal wardrobe

Save and name favourite looks under **Assign → Player**, then open the compact **Quick saved outfits** picker through its MCM binding. Your character can have travelling clothes, everyday wear and a collection of outfits worth returning to.

The personal wardrobe belongs to the loaded save. NPC and faction assignments are shared configuration used by saves loading those files. The [saves and backups guide](../ADORN_USER_DATA.md) explains what to preserve when moving or updating your installation.

## Share your choices

Assignment sharing exports contributions for other players to review. Imports show recipients, dependencies and conflicts before applying accepted changes; attribution travels with the contribution.

Packs carry assignments and item references. The original clothing and NPC mods supply their own assets and remain required. See [assignment sharing](../ASSIGNMENT_SHARING.md) for the review and merge workflow.

## How Adorn and ASF fit together

**Adorn works on its own** for outfit browsing, assignments and the ordinary player wardrobe. **Adorn Seller Framework (ASF)** is an optional companion mod that connects selected looks to a seller, an item-payment challenge and either finished-piece or learnable-recipe rewards.

If you want to dress the world, begin with Adorn. If you also want your character to earn their chosen looks through a seller service, read the [introduction to ASF](../ASF_INTRO.md).

The [compatibility and limits guide](../STATUS.md) explains the assignment bank, content compatibility and other practical limits.
