# Introduction to author tools

**Author tools let you create the content behind Adorn Seller Framework (ASF): the seller’s conversation, item-payment challenges, recipe rules and prepared audio.** They are for NPC authors, modlist creators and players building their own setup. Players using a finished seller pack can follow its instructions without preparing new dialogue themselves.

The tools work together. The seller gives the service a face, the conversation gives them a personality, the riddle defines the payment and the recipe defines what the player learns to craft. A prepared build connects the authored replies to native Skyrim dialogue.

## Begin with the seller

Read the [ASF introduction](../ASF_INTRO.md) to understand the player loop, then follow [seller setup](../ASF_MANUAL.md#author-setup). Choose a reachable placed NPC, enable the service and decide whether to allow finished-outfit orders or keep the setup focused on recipes.

Author access is unlocked through the MCM. It exposes configuration controls; it does not supply a new NPC, install the item dependencies or make a progression pool balanced automatically.

## Write the conversation and choose its performance

The [dialogue guide](../ASF_MANUAL.md#write-the-seller-dialogue) covers seven core exchanges and compatible alternative player prompts. Save separate drafts for the NPCs you author, with one active seller role at a time.

For each exchange, choose a stock recording, a selected personal take or silent preparation. The [recording guide](../ASF_MANUAL.md#record-or-import-audio) explains microphone capture, audio imports, waveform trimming and matching the recorded words to the reply.

## Decide how rewards are crafted

The [recipe maker](../ASF_MANUAL.md#author-recipe-templates) defines crafting materials per piece, parchment instructions and optional station locations. It supplies the rules used by new recipe orders; accepted orders retain their saved recipe.

This is separate from the riddle payment. A player can first solve the seller’s request to earn the note, then gather the recipe’s own materials to make each piece.

## Build a fair challenge pool

Start with the [first-riddle guide](../RIDDLE_TIERS_QUICK_GUIDE.md). Select real answer items and quantities, then write a riddle that fairly points to them. The [full riddle reference](../RIDDLE_AUTHORING.md) covers every field, the extra clue and the saved content format.

If you want reward progression, review [tiers and fixed offers](../ASF_MANUAL.md#riddle-maker-and-tier-configuration) before enabling the optional tier rules. Starter content is an example pool, not a balanced progression ready for every modlist.

[Writing assistance](../ASF_MANUAL.md#openrouter-author-assistance) is optional. It offers suggestions for your chosen answers; you remain responsible for reviewing and saving the final wording.

## Prepare, test and share

Use [Build and share a seller](../ASF_MANUAL.md#build-and-activate-dialogue) to turn saved drafts into matching dialogue records and audio. Changed native dialogue requires the build, game exit, installation and restart sequence.

Check the [author requirements](../SELLER_NEXUS_REQUIREMENTS.md#authoring-tools) before building. Play through the seller’s conversation, hand-in, clues and rewards in Skyrim before distributing the setup, and confirm that their ordinary NPC dialogue is still available.
